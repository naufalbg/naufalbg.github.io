About me

Welcome to my digital repository. This space serves as a meticulously curated archive to document, preserve, and showcase the research, projects, and major technical works I have undertaken throughout my academic journey—and hopefully more in the years to come.

I have always viewed every major course project as far more than just a mandatory lesson. For me, each one represents an open invitation to push my boundaries, broaden my technical horizons, and dive deeply into complex topics well beyond what is formally required in the syllabus.

Currently in my final year as an Electronics and Instrumentation student, my vision is firmly set on the future. I am actively building a long-term career deeply rooted in the energy sector. My specific passions and technical expertise align closely with the evolving demands of oil & gas, advanced mining operations, and the critical infrastructure of industrial energy systems.

Through the integration of cutting-edge tech, machine learning, and robust control systems, I strive to engineer solutions that make these vital industries safer, more efficient, and future-ready.
